Foundations of Quantum Computation 2025-26

MEI - MSc in Informatics Engineering

Dep. Informática, Universidade do Minho

Learning Outcomes

On successful completion of the course students should be able

  • To understand basic concepts of quantum information and quantum computation.
  • To master the quantum computational model.
  • To design, analyse and implement quantum algorithms.

Syllabus

  • Motivation: Quantum effects as computational resources
  • Part 1: Quantum information and computation.
    • From bits to qubits: Single-state quantum systems.
    • Superposition and interference.
    • Hilbert spaces and the quantum state postulates: representation, composition, evolution, and measurement.
    • Entanglement.
  • Part 2: Quantum Algorithms.
    • Quantum gates and the circuit model.
    • Query algorithms.
    • Classical computations over the quantum circuit model.
    • Algorithms based on phase amplification.
    • Algorithms based on the quantum Fourier transform.
    • Introduction to hybrid algorithms and quantum machine learning.
  • Part 3: Quantum programming in PennyLane.
